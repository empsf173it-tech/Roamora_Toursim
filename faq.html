Booking & Reservations
How do I book a tour with Roamora?
Booking is simple! Browse our packages on the Services or Destinations page, click "Book Now" or "Learn More" on any package, and fill out our inquiry form. A dedicated travel consultant will contact you within 2 hours to confirm availability, customize your itinerary, and process payment securely online.
How far in advance should I book my trip?
We recommend booking at least 3—6 months in advance for peak seasons (June—August and December—January) and popular destinations like Maldives, Bali, and European summer routes. For off-peak seasons, 4—8 weeks is usually sufficient. Last-minute bookings can sometimes be arranged — contact us to check availability.
What is included in the tour packages?
Package inclusions vary by tour type, but typically include: round-trip airfare, accommodation (hotel/resort), airport transfers, daily breakfast, guided activities, and a dedicated local guide. Premium packages also include all meals, private transportation, travel insurance, and exclusive experiences. Full inclusions are listed on each package page.
Cancellation & Refunds
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 60+ days before departure receive a full refund. 30—59 days: 75% refund. 15—29 days: 50% refund. Less than 15 days: non-refundable. We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance to protect against unexpected cancellations. Emergency situations are assessed case-by-case.
Can I modify my booking after confirmation?
Yes, modifications are possible up to 30 days before departure, subject to availability. Changes to dates, accommodation, or guest count may incur a modification fee of $50—$150 depending on the complexity. Contact your travel consultant as soon as possible for any changes.
Travel Documents
Do you assist with visa applications?
Yes! We provide full visa assistance for most destinations. Our team prepares documentation checklists, invitation letters, hotel/itinerary proof, and step-by-step application guidance. For complex visas (Schengen, USA, China), we partner with professional visa agencies. Visa fees are not included in tour prices unless stated.
Payments & Pricing
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, bank wire transfers, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum). Payments are processed through Stripe's secure SSL-encrypted platform. A 30% deposit is required at booking, with the balance due 60 days before departure.
Is there a best price guarantee?
Absolutely! If you find the exact same package cheaper elsewhere within 48 hours of booking, we'll match the price and give you an additional 10% discount. Simply email us with the competitor's quote and we'll verify and honor it. Certain restrictions apply — contact us for details.
Health & Safety
Is travel insurance included in packages?
Basic travel insurance is included in all our Premium and Luxury packages. Standard packages require you to purchase separate travel insurance, which we can arrange through our partner InsureMyTrip. We strongly recommend comprehensive coverage that includes medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and luggage protection.
What health precautions should I take before traveling?
We provide destination-specific health advisories for every booking. Recommended vaccinations, malaria prophylaxis, altitude sickness precautions, and food/water safety guidelines are shared in your pre-trip documentation. Always consult your doctor 4—6 weeks before departure, especially for destinations in Africa, South America, and Southeast Asia.
Custom & Group Travel
Can you create a custom itinerary for my group?
Absolutely — custom travel is our specialty! For groups of 4 or more, we design fully bespoke itineraries tailored to your interests, schedule, budget, and pace. From family reunions to corporate retreats and adventure groups, we handle every detail. Contact us with your requirements and we'll send a free custom quote within 48 hours.
Do you offer solo travel packages?
Yes! We have a dedicated Solo Traveler program with small-group tours (max 12 people) specifically designed for solo adventurers. No single supplement charges on select tours, built-in social opportunities, safety-first itineraries, and access to our solo traveler community. It's the best way to explore the world while meeting like-minded travelers.
